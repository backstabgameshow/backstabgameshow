Eligibility

Before you proceed, you must meet every requirement below. Honesty is rewarded here. Lying is too — but only if you make it to the end.

  • ✦ You are 16 years of age or older.
  • ✦ You have a stable internet connection. Disconnects mid-game can compromise the entire cast.
  • ✦ You have a PC, laptop, or device with a working camera and microphone. Built-in laptop mics are acceptable provided audio quality is clear.
  • ✦ You can play from a quiet, well-lit environment for the full duration without interruption.
  • ✦ You have the desktop version of Discord installed (not browser, not mobile) and an account in good standing.
  • ✦ You are comfortable being recorded and having your footage published publicly on YouTube.
  • ✦ You bring the spirit of the game. Backstab rewards scheming, alliance-building, and yes — deception. If you can't lie to a friend's face, this isn't for you.
  • ✦ You can commit to the full production window. Shoots run between 3:00 PM and 8:00 PM Eastern, with standard call time at 7:00 PM ET. Players arrive 30 minutes before game start. Expect 2.5 to 3 hours per game.
  • ✦ You agree to sign a standard release before filming, granting permission to publish your appearance.

The Audition

This is where we meet you.

Record a video between 2 and 3 minutes long using the exact setup you intend to play the game with — same camera, same mic, same lighting, same room.

We're looking for people who pop on camera: energy, presence, charisma, opinions. Tell us who you are, why you want to play, and what kind of player you'd be. Be entertaining. Be honest. Be specific.

Backstab is committed to casting a diverse, intersectional group of players. Every background, identity, and perspective is welcome — and encouraged.

Upload your video to YouTube (unlisted), Google Drive, Dropbox, or any platform that produces a shareable link. Paste the link below.
